        Console

        📹 Live drone feed

        Connect a drone receiver, capture card or phone camera. COCO-SSD runs on this device: green = person, orange = vehicle, blue = other. A person in view raises an alert and counts toward Survivors spotted.

        🧠 AI damage analysis

        Upload, paste or drop an image, or use the live frame. Gemini returns a structured briefing with a 0–100 risk score. You can raise an incident from it in one tap.

        🚨 Incidents & SOS

        Incidents broadcast instantly to every connected console. SOS raises a CRITICAL incident at your GPS position. Move incidents through Open → Responding → Resolved.

        🗺️ Routes

        Routes use OpenRouteService when configured, otherwise the free OSRM router. If both are unreachable you get a straight-line estimate, so a route is always available.

        🌦️ Weather risk

        Rain > 5 mm → flood risk HIGH. Wind > 40 km/h or gusts > 60 → HIGH. Temperature > 40 °C → heat MEDIUM. Drone flights are not advised above 30 km/h wind.

        ⌨️ Shortcuts

        1–9 switch panels · / focus assistant · Esc close dialogs · Ctrl+V paste an image on the Analysis panel.
